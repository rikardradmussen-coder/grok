The rebuild

“I woke up alone in a house that felt like a tomb. I could not look at myself in the mirror.”

My kids were grown. The marriage was over. I had no idea who I was.

So I made a decision. Not a resolution. Not a promise to a therapist. I started walking. Then running. Then lifting. I changed what I ate. I built a day I could keep — and I learned the process instead of performing a speech.

Health is parental capacity. The file is how you stay in the room. Today I take five men at a time through the same fire.

If I can do it after 50, so can you.

The system

90 days. Three movements.

Not a content dump. A sequence. Ash, ember, fire — stop the bleed, write the file, lock the man they meet.

Days 1–30

Ash

Stop the bleed

Sleep, food, and a walk. Start the fact log. Cut the anaesthetic. Get a day you can survive without lying to yourself.

Days 31–60

Ember

Install the man

Training that progresses. Templates you actually send. Father presence. A mind that is no longer on trial all day.

Days 61–90

Fire

Lock the standard

The protocol becomes default. The file is clean. The man you keep when nobody is watching.

Three pillars

Process. File. Body.

01

Process

Know the room

Safeguarding letter. First hearing. Section 7. CIAF. Child in Need. Section 47. Cafcass is not social care. You show up as the man they actually meet.

02

File

CAFCASS has a file. So do you.

Contemporaneous. Dated. No adjectives. The seven emails and the fact log are how a clean record gets written — not a manifesto, not a late-night rant.

03

Body

Health is parental capacity

Sleep, protein, training you will not negotiate. The FCA is looking at whether you can meet the child's needs. The body is evidence.
