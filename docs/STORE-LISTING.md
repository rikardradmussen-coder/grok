## Full description
Project Phoenix is a 90-day coaching help group for men in England and Wales who are in a live family-court and social-care process.

Five men at a time. Health, a usable file, and steady contact with the children — while legal strategy stays with your solicitor.

What you get
• Orientation on the room you are actually in (Cafcass is not social care)
• Tools for a calm, dated file — not late-night rants
• Body and mind standards that support parental capacity
• Phoenix Council coaches for day-to-day standards between calls

Coaching only — not legal advice. We do not replace a solicitor. We do not tell anyone how to “win” a case. No outcome promises.

If you or a child is in immediate danger, call 999. If you are in crisis, Samaritans is 116 123. We are not that service.
