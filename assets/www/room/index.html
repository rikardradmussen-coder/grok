Who is who

Do not mix the doors

England

Cafcass

Independent. Advises the family court on the child's welfare. Not the judge. Not your solicitor. Not social services.

Wales

Cafcass Cymru

The Welsh equivalent. Same job, different organisation. Do not mix the two in your notes.

Local authority

Children's social care

Assesses need and risk in the community. Child in Need, Section 47, child protection. A different door from Cafcass.

England & Wales

Family court

Decides. Cafcass advises. You show up as the man they actually meet that day.

Children Act 1989

The test is not fairness

Paramountcy — s1(1)

The child's welfare is the test. Not fairness to you. Not fathers' rights.

No delay — s1(2)

Time is a harm. Do not invent process. Keep the next date.

Involvement — s1(2A)

A presumption of parental involvement. Not equal time. It yields to risk.

PD12J

Domestic abuse is a welfare issue. Fact-finding if needed. Do not reframe allegations.

CMS is not this room

Child maintenance is usually the Child Maintenance Service. Do not fight money in the welfare hearing.

Private law

Child arrangements

  1. 01

    MIAM

    Mediation Information and Assessment Meeting first, unless a genuine exemption. A solicitor decides the exemption — not you, not this page.

  2. 02

    C100 issued

    The court sends it to Cafcass. Forms (C100, C1A, C7, C8) are named here. They are completed with a solicitor.

  3. 03

    Safeguarding checks

    Police and local-authority checks. A Family Court Adviser usually phones the adults. They do not usually speak to the child at this stage.

  4. 04

    Safeguarding letter / FHDRA

    A short letter, typically before the first hearing. Safety first — not who is the better parent. At the FHDRA: answer what is asked. Then stop.

  5. 05

    Section 7 report

    If the case continues, Cafcass or the local authority advises using the s1(3) welfare checklist. Paramountcy is s1(1). Involvement is not equal time.

  6. 06

    Rule 16.4 guardian

    If the child is made a party, a children's guardian is appointed. Heavier than a Section 7. Do not treat them as the same. s37 is a different weight again.

Social care

The local authority

  1. 01

    Early help / Child in Need (s17)

    Support. Not an accusation. Working Together 2023 is the framework. Engage. Write names, dates, what was said.

  2. 02

    Section 47 enquiry

    Reasonable cause to suspect significant harm. They do not need your permission to enquire. Stay calm. Solicitor the same day.

  3. 03

    Child protection conference

    Multi-agency. Categories: neglect, physical, sexual, emotional. Facts in that room. No attacking the other parent.

  4. 04

    Child protection plan

    What must change, who does what, when it is reviewed. Keep the log. Keep the standard.

  5. 05

    PLO / pre-proceedings

    Letter before proceedings. The Public Law Outline. 26-week care proceedings if issued. Threshold for a care order is s31 significant harm.

  6. 06

    Care or supervision

    The local authority is in court. Cafcass often acts as children's guardian. That is not a private-law s7. s38 ICO, s44 EPO, s46 police protection — solicitor the same day.

Children Act 1989

The welfare checklist

The child's welfare is paramount. This is what the room is looking at. Not a scorecard.

  1. 01The child's wishes and feelings, given their age and understanding
  2. 02Physical, emotional and educational needs
  3. 03The likely effect of any change in circumstances
  4. 04Age, sex, background and any characteristics the court considers relevant
  5. 05Any harm suffered or at risk of suffering
  6. 06How capable each parent is of meeting the child's needs
  7. 07The powers available to the court

The work

How you show up

  • On time. Sober. Phone away. Clothes that say adult, not performance.
  • Date, time, who, what happened. No adjectives. No manifesto.
  • Do not badmouth the other parent to the child, the FCA, or the social worker.
  • Do not coach the child on what to say.
  • Answer the question asked. Then stop.
  • If a report is wrong on facts: the solicitor writes. You do not email the FCA at midnight.
  • Never ignore a court order.
