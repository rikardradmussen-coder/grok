7 emails that land in the file

A guide, not a trick. Child-focused. Dated. Short. They beat a rant. They do not replace a solicitor. Not legal advice.

How to use them

The file has rules

  1. 01

    Child first

    If the sentence is not about the child's week, do not send it. The marriage does not go in this thread.

  2. 02

    One fact per line

    Date, time, place, what happened. No adjectives. No theory. CIAF is looking at impact on the child — not who won the email.

  3. 03

    One ask

    Confirm, choose A or B, or stop. Do not litigate. A second message is usually the rant.

  4. 04

    Then log it

    Copy. Fill the brackets. Send once. Put the same facts in The Room. That is the file.

  5. 05

    Solicitor first

    Anything to an FCA, Cafcass, or the court goes through your solicitor if you have one. Template 06 is not a secret channel.

  6. 06

    Initials are enough

    In this app, in The Room, in the Council. Full names belong in the real file, not a chat tool.
